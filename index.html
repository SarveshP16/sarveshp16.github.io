02 // secure delivery

Every commit runs the gauntlet.

The pipeline I build into every team. Each gate blocks on failure — nothing reaches production unscanned, unsigned or out of policy.

  1. Commit

    Signed commits, secret scanning on push, pre-commit hooks.

  2. SAST

    Semgrep and CodeQL on every PR, tuned until the noise is gone.

  3. SCA & SBOM

    Dependency audit, licence checks, an SBOM for every build.

  4. Image scan

    Trivy gates on critical CVEs. Distroless bases by default.

  5. Policy

    OPA checks IaC and manifests before anything is applied.

  6. Sign & deploy

    Cosign signatures, admission control rejects anything unsigned.
